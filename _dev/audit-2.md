# Second audit of adrftools (2026-11-13)

A second pass over `R/`, the documentation, the vignette, and the README, after every issue in `_dev/audit.md` except M19 was resolved. Four reviewers covered the estimation pipeline (`adrf.R`, `get_estimator.r`, `gradient.R`, the first half of `aux_functions.R`), the helpers and inference code (the second half of `aux_functions.R`, `utils.R`, `posterior_inference.R`), the curve modifiers (`effect_curve.R`, `amef.R`, `curve_contrast.R`, `reference_curve.R`, `point_contrast.R`, `curve_projection.R`), and summaries, plots, printing, and documentation. Each reproduced its findings in R, and the code findings marked ✓ were reproduced again independently (scripts in the session's temporary directory; the key calls are quoted below). Findings marked ○ were reproduced only by the reviewer.

Issues are numbered R1, R2, ... for reference. Line numbers are as of this audit.

## Performance

- **~~R32. Bootstrap and posterior covariances are recomputed from the draws.~~** ~~○ Evaluating a bootstrapped or Bayesian curve, `point_contrast()`, and `reference_curve()` compute `cov()` of the transformed draws, but the map is linear, so `quad_mult(L, cov(draws))` is identical and about 37 times faster (5.1 s to 0.14 s for 1000 points with 4000 draws).~~ Fixed: the covariance is computed as `quad_mult(L, vcov)` in `.make_fun()`, curve evaluation, `point_contrast()`, and `reference_curve()`; tests check equality with the covariance of the transformed replicates.
- **R33. Simultaneous Wald p-values take seconds to minutes.** ✓ One `pmvt()` call per estimate with `maxpts = 1e6`: 23 s for 51 estimates against 0.01 s pointwise. `maxpts = 1e5, abseps = 1e-4` was 12 times faster with p-values within 8e-4 (○), and skipping estimates whose Bonferroni bound is far below any reported precision would cut it further.
- **R34. `summary()` and `plot()` compute simultaneous critical values with different settings.** ✓ `summary.curve_est()` passes `maxpts = 1e6`; `plot()` and `point_contrast()` pass `keepAttr` and `maxiter`, which are not arguments of `qmvt()`'s Monte Carlo algorithm, so they use the default `maxpts = 25000`. With 51 points, `summary()` took 8 s and `plot()` 0.3 s, and their limits differed by up to 4.5e-4 (○). Fix: one helper with shared settings.
- **~~R35. Models with offsets take the slow general path.~~** ~~✓ `model.matrix()` omits offsets, so the fast-path check fails: a Poisson model with an offset took 0.97 s against 0.06 s without (15 times). Fix: add `model.offset()` to the linear predictor in the fast path.~~ Fixed: `.grid_offset()` evaluates `offset()` terms and an `offset` argument on the grid, and the offset is added in the routing check, the estimators, and the analytic gradient. Offset models now take the fast path (0.02 s against 1 s) with estimates and SEs matching `predict()` and a numerical delta method.
- **R36. Character predictors with `subset` take the slow path.** ○ `model.matrix()` drops levels absent from the subset; 27 times slower than with a factor. Fix: pass `xlev = x$xlevels`.
- **R37. `method = "sim"` builds a dense matrix for a diagonal covariance.** ○ 1.6 GB and 2.6 s for 70 eigenvalues and 1e6 draws; summing `ev[k] * rnorm(nsim)^2` in chunks takes 1.2 s and 60 MB with the same p-value.
- **R38. The fast path holds O(N × n × p) memory, and `estfun()` and `bread()` run twice.** ○ The `?adrf` example's model matrix is 86 MB per copy, and `.get_grad_fun.glm` makes a second copy; building it one treatment value at a time would cut the peak by a factor of n. The validity check in `process_vcov_and_cluster()` computes and discards `estfun()` and `bread()` (about 40% of `adrf()` time for an M-estimation `glm_weightit` fit).
- **R39. Bootstrap and posterior omnibus tests refit the projection once per draw even with the identity transformation.** ○ Minor (0.15 s for 4000 draws against 0.002 s with a residual-maker matrix).

## Not issues, or deliberate

The reviewers confirmed:
- contrast matrices against hand computations for `curve_contrast()`, `point_contrast()`, and `reference_curve()`;
- bootstrap and posterior chains of modifiers;
- `anova()` against a hand Wald F;
- the exact simultaneous posterior bands and their p-value inversion;
- `quad_mult()`, `.to_psd()`, `pool_est()`, `.svyglm_vcov()`, and `get_locpoly_w()` accuracy (away from the scale problems in R5);
- *svyglm*, *WeightIt*, multiply imputed, and *rstanarm* fits with `by`, `subset`, and `wts`;
- numerical against analytic gradients.

The `range` quantiles are computed over the full sample rather than the subset or subgroup, which may be intended but is not documented.
